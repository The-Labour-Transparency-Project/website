## The proposition

New Zealand agriculture already traces products, food-safety inputs and environmental performance with increasing precision. The workforce that makes production possible is often far less visible.

The project asks a practical question: **if a product can be traced to its origin, can the work behind it be traced too?**

<div class="diagram">
  <div><strong>Worker</strong>Identity, agreement and right to work.</div>
  <div><strong>Employer</strong>Clear legal accountability.</div>
  <div><strong>Labour provider</strong>Declared contracting and subcontracting relationships.</div>
  <div><strong>Production site</strong>Attribution of work to place and period.</div>
  <div><strong>Supply chain</strong>Assurance and market participation built on trustworthy records.</div>
</div>

This is not a proposal for a single centralised platform. It is a standards-led approach that can work across payroll, onboarding, contractor-management and assurance systems.
