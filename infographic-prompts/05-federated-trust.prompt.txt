Use case: editorial infographic.

Create a one-page vertical infographic for The Labour Transparency Project titled:
“Federated Trust: Distributed Participation, Coordinated Assurance.”

Subtitle: “Trust is distributed. Assurance is coordinated.”

Audience: sponsors, industry participants, regulators, workers’ representatives and researchers.

Format and style:
- 1080 × 1350 px, 4:5 vertical layout.
- Warm ivory background; calm, rigorous, modern institutional style.
- Flat vector-like geometry, clear hierarchy, generous whitespace and excellent mobile readability.
- Palette: deep navy #102A43, forest green #1F5B52, muted ochre #B58A3B, neutral grey #9A9E9A.
- No logos, product names, trademarks, partner names or invented statistics.

Core message: no single participant needs to own all workforce data. Each participant can retain control of source records while sharing authorised claims, attestations, references and signals that enable wider assurance.

Visual structure:
1. A central ring labelled “Coordinated assurance”. Around it, neutral participant nodes: workers, employers, contractors, labour providers, auditors, regulators, industry bodies and systems of record.
2. Show that participants retain source records while trusted references or verified claims travel across legitimate relationships.
3. A four-part explanation: “Source authority”, “Delegated verification”, “Proportional access”, “Shared accountability”.
4. A comparison strip: “Centralise everything” versus “Coordinate what can be trusted”.

Network lines must show legitimate relationships and information boundaries, not a generic technology network. Do not use literal chains, maps, globes, shields or surveillance imagery.

Footer line: “Federation lets assurance travel without requiring every participant to surrender control.”

Ensure all text is exact, legible and spelled correctly.
