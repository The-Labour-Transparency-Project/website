Use case: editorial infographic.

Create a one-page vertical infographic for The Labour Transparency Project titled:
“Product Traceability Is Mature. Workforce Traceability Is Not.”

Subtitle: “The next governance gap in responsible supply chains.”

Audience: sponsors, industry participants, regulators, workers’ representatives and researchers. Ground the examples initially in New Zealand agriculture and horticulture, while making the message globally transferable.

Format and style:
- 1080 × 1350 px, 4:5 vertical layout.
- Warm ivory or white background; calm institutional editorial design.
- Flat vector-like geometry, clean typography, generous whitespace and strong mobile readability.
- Palette: deep navy #102A43, forest green #1F5B52, muted ochre #B58A3B, neutral grey #9A9E9A.
- No logos, product names, trademarks, partner names or invented statistics.

Core message: industries have engineered increasingly precise product traceability, while the workforce relationships behind production often remain fragmented, retrospective and difficult to verify.

Visual structure:
1. Hero comparison: a clear, orderly product pathway from inputs to production to distribution, beside a fragmented workforce pathway involving employers, contractors, labour providers, subcontractors and workers.
2. Three questions beneath the workforce side: “Who is connected to whom?”, “Where is each worker attributed?”, and “Can an event be traced and contained?”
3. A transition panel: “From documentation to infrastructure.” Show that workforce traceability requires connected relationships, attributable records, event-level visibility and accountable governance.
4. Closing panel: “The next stage of supply-chain governance includes the people who make production possible.”

Use small abstract nodes and relationship lines only where they clarify the system. Do not use literal chains, a globe, a shield, surveillance imagery or worker stereotypes. Keep people represented as simple neutral participant symbols, not portraits.

Footer line: “Products are traceable. The workforces producing them must become visible too.”

Ensure all text is exact, legible and spelled correctly.
